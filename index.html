Talking Videos

Filming Style

  • Eye-level angle, natural light
  • Conversational, never over-performed
  • Minimal editing, authentic delivery
  • Ideally 30–90 seconds

Energy

Thoughtful · calm · curious · reflective. Never scripted, salesy, or influencer-style.
